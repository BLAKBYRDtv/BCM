The idea

An open Word, a lit cross, a people who choose.

The mark puts a gold cross inside a ring above an open Bible, held between a navy B and M. Navy carries the authority of the house. Gold carries the Word and the light of the cross. Everything else in the identity is built from those two.

Logo suite

One mark, four ways to use it.

PrimaryWhite and pearl grounds, print and documents.
ReversedMidnight and navy grounds, video and the website.
MonogramHeaders, stamps and small spaces.
Cross markFavicon, app icon and loading screens.
  • Clear spaceKeep a margin equal to the width of the gold cross on every side.
  • Minimum sizeFull logo 160 px wide on screen. Below that, use the monogram or the cross.
  • On photographyDarken the photo under the logo and use the reversed version.
  • NeverStretch, rotate, recolor, outline, or place it on gold or bright blue.

Rooted

Honest

Real facts only. No invented times, addresses or staff until the church confirms them.
